Work Hours

Is it open right now?

Work Hours keeps the opening hours of the places you visit in one list: the bakery, the post office, the dentist. It shows today's date and, for every place, whether it is open right now (in green, with today's hours) or closed. Each place keeps its hours, a photo, an address with a map, and its website, email and phone.

Support

Questions, a bug or an idea? Email info@ost-consultancy.nl. Please mention your device and iOS version. You will get an answer within a few working days.

How do I use it?

  • Add a place with +, and find one by name with search.
  • In the editor, Add Hours sets an opening and closing time and the days. Add several for a lunch break or late evenings; hours past midnight work too.
  • Open a place and tap the address for the map, or the website, email or phone to use them.
  • Swipe a row to the left to edit or delete it, or open a place and tap Edit.

I updated from an older version. Where are my places?

Version 2.1 opens the same data as the earlier versions, so your places, hours and photos are where you left them. If something is missing, write to us before deleting the app.

Is my data backed up?

Your places live only on your iPhone and are part of its iCloud or computer backup.

Privacy policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Work Hours does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Everything you enter, including photos, is saved only on your device and deleted when you delete the app.
  • The camera is used only when you take a photo of a place, and the photo stays on your device.
  • When you open the map of a place, its address is sent to Apple's map service to find it on the map. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
