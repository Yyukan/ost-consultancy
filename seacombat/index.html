Sea Combat

Turn-based naval board game for iPhone and iPad

Two fleets of eight ships face each other, each behind its own row of mines. Clear a way through, outmanoeuvre the enemy and take every enemy ship off the board. No dice: the same position always gives the same result. Play against a friend on one device, or against the computer at three levels.

Support

Questions, a bug or an idea? Email info@ost-consultancy.nl. Please mention your device and iOS version. You will get an answer within a few working days.

Where are the rules?

In the game: tap the round three-line button on the blue plate, then Rules.

How do I move?

Tap one of your ships, then tap one of the markers that show where it can go, and pass the device.

I bought the game on another device. How do I get it here?

Open the App Store with the same Apple Account and download Sea Combat again. You don't pay twice.

Privacy policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Sea Combat does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The game does not connect to the internet.
  • Your game settings, such as the opponent and level you picked, are saved only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple's privacy policy applies to them; the developer receives no personal information about buyers.

The game is suitable for children and collects no data from them either.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Questions about privacy: info@ost-consultancy.nl.
