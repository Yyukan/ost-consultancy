Heli Combat 1967

Retro chopper rescue game for iPhone and iPad

A top-down helicopter rescue game. Fly a Cobra attack chopper over six hand-made jungle islands, bomb open the huts and winch eight downed pilots to safety on each. Guns, tanks, trains, warships, enemy choppers and jets all shoot back, and fuel is your clock. No ads, no in-app purchases, works offline.

How do I fly and shoot?

Touch the left half of the screen and push: a floating stick appears and the Cobra turns and flies where you push. Hold GUN to fire, tap BOMB to drop a bomb. The yellow sight shows where it lands.

How do I rescue the pilots?

Hover low over a pilot until they climb the rope ladder. Pilots locked in fenced huts must be bombed out first. Up to eight fit on board; land on the H to drop them off.

I'm low on fuel, armour or ammo. What now?

Land on the H. It refuels, repairs and re-arms the Cobra, paid for with points.

How do I pause?

Tap the pause button at the top right.

Why can't I pick a later island?

Islands unlock in order. Rescue the pilots on an island to unlock the next one.

How do I turn the sound off?

Open Settings from the start screen. The game also follows the silent switch.

I bought the game on another device. How do I get it here?

Open the App Store with the same Apple Account and download Heli Combat 1967 again. You don't pay twice.

Privacy policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Heli Combat 1967 does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The game does not connect to the internet.
  • Your game progress, the islands you unlocked, your best score for each one and the sound setting, is saved only on your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple's privacy policy applies to them; the developer receives no personal information about buyers.

The game collects no data from children either.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
