Wiki Quoter

Random quotes from Wikiquote for iPhone

Wiki Quoter hangs one quotation at a time on the wall, in a hand-drawn picture frame. Every quote comes from a random page of Wikiquote, in English, Spanish or Ukrainian. There are no buttons, only gestures.

Support

Questions, a bug or an idea? Email info@ost-consultancy.nl. Please mention your device and iOS version. You will get an answer within a few working days.

How do I use it?

  • Swipe left for the next quote, swipe right to go back.
  • Swipe down to pick the edition: English, Español or Українська.
  • Tap the author to open the Wikiquote page the quote came from.
  • Double tap the author to hide them for good and turn to a new page.
  • Long press the quote to share it.

I hid an author by mistake. How do I get them back?

Hidden authors are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears the list.

Nothing loads.

The quotes come live from wikiquote.org, so the app needs an internet connection. Swipe to try again.

Privacy policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Wiki Quoter does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • To show quotes, the app downloads random pages from wikiquote.org. Like any website, Wikiquote receives the technical details of that request, such as your IP address; the Wikimedia privacy policy applies to it. The app sends nothing else.
  • Your settings, the edition you picked and the authors you hid, are saved only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Tapping an author opens Wikiquote in Safari, where Safari's and Wikimedia's policies apply.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
