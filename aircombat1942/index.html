Air Combat 1942

Retro WWII arcade shooter for iPhone and iPad

A classic vertical-scrolling shoot 'em up. Fly a P-38 Lightning over jungle, desert, coastline and open sea through ten hand-made missions. Fighters, tanks, trains and ships all shoot back, and every mission ends with a boss. Power up from one gun to a full squadron with wingmen. No ads, no in-app purchases, works offline.

Support

Questions, a bug or an idea? Email info@ost-consultancy.nl. Please mention your device and iOS version. You will get an answer within a few working days.

How do I fly and shoot?

Drag anywhere on the screen. The plane follows your finger without sitting under it, and the guns fire on their own.

How do I pause?

Tap the pause button at the top right.

Why can't I pick a later mission?

Missions unlock in order. Finish a mission, including its boss, to unlock the next one.

I bought the game on another device. How do I get it here?

Open the App Store with the same Apple Account and download Air Combat 1942 again. You don't pay twice.

Privacy policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Air Combat 1942 does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The game does not connect to the internet.
  • Your game progress, the missions you unlocked and your best score for each one, is saved only on your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple's privacy policy applies to them; the developer receives no personal information about buyers.

The game collects no data from children either.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Questions about privacy: info@ost-consultancy.nl.
