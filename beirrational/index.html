Be Irrational

A wish list for the things you would do now

Be Irrational is a notepad for what you would like to do, not a schedule. Group your wishes into scopes of your life, keep the ones you would do now on the Now list, put the rest aside for someday, and watch the progress rings fill up.

Support

Questions, a bug or an idea? Email info@ost-consultancy.nl. Please mention your device and iOS version. You will get an answer within a few working days.

How do I use it?

  • Add a scope of your life with +, or pick one of the suggested scopes.
  • Open a scope and add what you would do now.
  • Open an occupation to set its progress; the ring shows how far you are.
  • Swipe a row to the left to move it to Someday, Completed or Trash; swipe right to edit it.
  • Edit lets you reorder or remove scopes and occupations.

I updated from an older version. Where are my lists?

Version 1.3 opens the same data as the earlier versions, so everything is where you left it. If something is missing, write to us before deleting the app.

Is my data backed up?

Your lists live only on your device and are part of its iCloud or computer backup.

Privacy policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Be Irrational does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.
  • Everything you write in it is saved only on your device and deleted when you delete the app.
